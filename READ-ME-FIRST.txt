ADULTING  v2026.08.16e — Worker URL baked in

WHAT CHANGED
  Your Worker URL is now hardcoded as the built-in default:
      https://bluebonnetproxy.dustin12342986.workers.dev

  Blue Bonnet no longer depends on anything being saved in Settings. Clear your
  site data, switch browsers, use a new device — it still works.

  Settings still overrides it if you ever point it somewhere else.

WHY
  That URL had to be re-entered repeatedly and kept not sticking. Three
  different causes, all producing the identical "Couldn't reach the assistant"
  message:
    - clearing site data wiped it
    - Settings has three separate forms with three Save buttons, so pasting in
      one and clicking another silently discarded it
    - one paste picked up a whole paragraph of surrounding text

  There was nothing to gain from keeping it configurable — it's your own
  Worker and the URL is visible in network requests anyway.

  A second bug was found while testing this: the "do we have Anthropic?" check
  compared the URL against the fallback constant. Turning that constant into a
  real URL would have made a perfectly good default look unconfigured. It now
  checks whether the URL is actually a URL.

CHECKING IT YOURSELF
  Browser console:  BlueBonnet.status()
  Returns the URL in use, whether it's usable, whether the gateway key is
  filled in, and whether it came from Settings or the built-in default.
