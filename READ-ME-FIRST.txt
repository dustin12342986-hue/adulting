BUDGETING  (Budget tab, at the top)
  Import a statement, then "See my budget options". You get four plans built
  from YOUR spending, not generic percentages:
    - Match what I actually spend   (no cuts; a budget you can't fail)
    - Trim the easy stuff           (eating out, shopping, subscriptions)
    - Save meaningfully             (a real change you'd notice)
    - 50/30/20                      (only offered if income is known)

  Uses the MEDIAN month, not the average, so one car repair doesn't make every
  normal month look like a failure. Fixed costs (rent, insurance) are never
  "trimmed" — cuts come from flexible categories, and no category is ever set
  to zero.

  Once set, the top of Budget shows ONE number: safe to spend today. Any
  category can be edited inline.

  Blue Bonnet can build and adjust budgets by chat, and read your real numbers
  to tell you how the month is going.

ANTI-BURNOUT  (the week-2 dropoff)
  - No streaks. A cumulative count that only goes up and never resets.
  - Away 4+ days? The dashboard opens with a calm welcome and AT MOST three
    things, not a wall of red.
  - Always exactly one "Start here" suggestion, picked for you — usually the
    nearly-finished thing, because finishing restarts momentum.
  - "Under two minutes each" list for when starting is the hard part.
  - "Worth a look" is now "When you have a minute".
  - If a budget category keeps breaking, the app says the number is probably
    wrong, not you.
