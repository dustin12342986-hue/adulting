WHY STATEMENTS WERE ONLY HALF-READ
  Regex can't keep up with bank layouts. A pattern tuned for one bank drops
  most rows of another — on a real statement it found 7 of 200. Statements are
  now read by Claude through your Blue Bonnet Worker instead, returning every
  transaction with a readable merchant name and a category.

ALSO UPDATE YOUR WORKER  (this one matters for cost)
  Your Worker had the model hardcoded and ignored what the app asked for, so
  every call ran on the expensive model. Paste cloudflare-worker.js over your
  Worker's code and Deploy. Extraction then runs on Haiku, which does this
  mechanical job just as well for a fraction of the price.

TOKEN USE
  - Boilerplate, marketing, page headers and balance summaries are stripped
    before anything is sent. A line with no amount in it can't be a
    transaction.
  - Sent in large chunks, so the system prompt is repeated as few times as
    possible.
  - max_tokens is sized to the chunk instead of a big round number.
  - Charge Tracker sends a per-merchant roll-up, not raw lines: 200
    transactions become ~25 lines. Same information, a fifth of the tokens.
  Ballpark: a cent or two per statement.

BUDGET -> STATEMENTS
  Categories   Default. Every charge in a category, biggest first, with totals,
               % bars and counts. Click one to see the individual charges with
               real merchant names and the original text underneath.
  Recurring    What repeats, how often, next expected date.
  All          Everything.
  Charge Tracker  Written walkthrough from Blue Bonnet, with duplicates and
                  forgotten subscriptions flagged.
