WHY YOUR STATEMENTS LOOKED HALF-READ
  Not the page count — all pages were being read. Three parsing bugs were
  dropping or corrupting rows:
    1. Running BALANCE column was being taken as the transaction amount, so
       amounts were wrong on any statement with a balance column.
    2. Sections like "DEPOSITS AND ADDITIONS" print amounts unsigned; those
       rows were being read with the wrong sign or skipped.
    3. Two identical charges on one day (two coffees) were treated as a
       duplicate and the second was silently thrown away.
  Also fixed: descriptions that wrap onto a second line are now joined, and
  column headers / page footers are ignored.

NEW
  Recurring tab      Default view. Groups charges by merchant (ignoring store
                     numbers and city), works out the rhythm, and predicts the
                     next date. "+ Bill" turns any of them into a tracked bill.
  All transactions   The raw rows, behind a tab, with a clean merchant name on
                     top and the original description underneath.
  Charge Tracker     Sends your transactions to Blue Bonnet for a full
                     line-by-line breakdown: categories with totals and
                     percentages, what the cryptic merchants actually are,
                     repeating charges called out, possible duplicates and
                     forgotten subscriptions, and the few changes that would
                     actually matter.
